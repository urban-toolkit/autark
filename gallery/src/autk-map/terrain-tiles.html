Elevation streams as nested windows around the view: the finest covers a few kilometres, each further one twice the area at half the detail. Coarse tiles load first, so a surface appears quickly and sharpens. The square outline is a vector layer draped on the terrain. The request count includes retries and is not a count of network transfers: the browser may answer from its cache.
